## 1. SITE-WIDE OBSERVATIONS

### Overall feel

- Simple, bold, clean
- Black and white dominant palette
- Heavy uppercase typography for all headings and navigation
- Very minimal decorative elements
- Full-width hero images on every page
- Strong contrast between black text and white/off-white backgrounds
- Footer uses a warm off-white/cream background (#f5f1ec approximate)
- Site does not require login to browse any page
- Login is available via "MY ACCOUNT" in nav linking to external booking platform (Momence)

### Typography observations

- Logo: Serif font, light weight — "Pilates Republic."
- All nav items: ALL CAPS, wide letter spacing, sans-serif, small size
- All page headings: BOLD ALL CAPS, large, condensed sans-serif
- Body copy: Mixed case, regular weight, smaller size, readable gray tone
- Section labels: small, caps, spaced — used above section headings as eyebrows
- CTA links: ALL CAPS with arrow → or underline style
- Strong typographic hierarchy: large bold headline → smaller regular body

### Color palette observations

- Primary text: near-black (#1a1a1a approximate)
- Background: white (#ffffff) on content pages
- Footer background: warm cream / off-white (#f5f1ec approximate)
- Accent / muted color: warm taupe/rose-gray used for eyebrow labels and secondary links
- Hero overlay: white text directly on dark photograph, no overlay box
- Button/CTA: no traditional button box — text links with arrows or underlines only
- No visible bright accent colors — all very restrained and neutral

### Spacing observations

- Generous vertical padding between sections
- Wide max-width content container, nearly full viewport
- Very clean section separation — no borders, just whitespace
- Images and text alternate left/right or stack centered

### Header observations

- Sticky or fixed top header
- Logo: top left, serif, small
- Navigation: horizontal, all caps, spaced, center or right aligned
- No visible hamburger on desktop
- No prominent book button in header — MY ACCOUNT links to external booking platform
- Navigation items: LOCATIONS / CLASSES / PRICING / PRIVATE TRAINING / TEACHER TRAINING / REBEL RULES / FRANCHISE / MY ACCOUNT
- Locations has a dropdown revealing individual studio location links

### Footer observations

- Warm cream/off-white background — clearly different from page body
- Left side: copyright, phone, email
- Below: each studio location listed by name with full address and Google Maps link
- Social links: Facebook | Instagram — text links separated by pipe
- Right side: Employment Opportunities | Class Policies | Privacy Policy | Contact — text links separated by pipe
- No large logo in footer
- No columns layout — mostly linear stacked text
- Very simple but complete contact information

## 12. PAGE STRUCTURE PATTERNS

### Pattern shared across pages

- Header always same
- Hero: full-width image with white bold text overlay
- Content sections: generous padding, white background, centered or near-full-width containers
- Footer: always same, cream background

### Hero image treatment

- Full viewport width
- No dark overlay layer — text relies on naturally dark areas of photo or has sufficient contrast
- Text placed bottom-left or center depending on slide
- All-caps, bold, large white text

### CTA style on site

- Most CTAs are text links with → arrow
- All caps
- No traditional rectangular button with background fill on most pages
- Some purchase CTAs on pricing page are clickable card blocks rather than text buttons
