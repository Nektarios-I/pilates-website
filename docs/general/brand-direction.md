# Brand Direction — Corehouse Pilates Studio

This document presents 2–3 plausible aesthetic directions for the Pilates studio website. It is a decision aid, not an approval record.

**Important**: no direction is approved yet. Direction 2, **Warm Elegance**, is a provisional working candidate only. Later docs must not treat it as final unless a human explicitly approves it.

**Purpose**: choose one direction as the foundation for design tokens and the visual system.

**Constraints**: avoid generic SaaS aesthetics, purple gradients, template-like three-column grids, and placeholder imagery. Prefer restrained color, strong typography, clear hierarchy, and authentic content.

---

## Direction 1: Refined Minimalist

### Mood

- **Keywords**: Serene, sophisticated, authoritative, understated luxury
- **Tone**: Calm yet confident; speaks to precision and control
- **Impression**: High-end wellness studio with mastery of craft; minimal distraction, maximum focus
- **Best for**: Studios targeting professionals, serious practitioners, and those seeking premium refinement

### Typography

- **Headings**: Clean sans-serif, geometric (for example Inter or a similar grotesk)
- **Body**: Same family or a warmer companion such as Figtree or Graphik
- **Accent**: Optional subtle serif for restrained emphasis

### Color Direction

- Off-white or cream base
- Deep charcoal or near-black text
- Soft neutral accent such as warm gray or muted sage
- Very limited use of terracotta or bronze for calls to action

### Imagery Direction

- Clean, well-lit studio spaces
- Close-ups of movement and posture
- Natural light, muted tones, minimal post-processing
- Avoid generic stock imagery and heavily filtered photos

### Spacing and Layout

- Very open and spacious
- Favor 2–3 column layouts over dense grids
- Large vertical section spacing
- Narrower container width to keep focus

### Motion and Interaction

- Slow, gentle transitions
- Minimal microinteractions
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion

### Why This Direction Fits Pilates

- Mirrors precision, control, and intentionality
- Supports a calm, focused wellness tone
- Communicates expertise without visual noise

### What to Avoid

- Busy texture
- Colorful accent systems
- Playful or casual tone
- Oversized cinematic hero sections

### Reference Sites to Study

1. Reformer Pilates studio sites with restrained layouts
2. Premium wellness brands with calm typography
3. High-end coaching or personal training sites with subtle hierarchy

## Direction 2: Warm Elegance

### Mood

- **Keywords**: Welcoming, warm, sophisticated, grounded, approachable
- **Tone**: Professional and expert, but not cold
- **Impression**: Upscale wellness community where precision meets warmth
- **Best for**: Studios that want broad appeal and a stronger human tone

### Typography

- **Headings**: Serif typeface such as Crimson Text or Lora
- **Body**: Warm sans-serif such as Figtree, Poppins, or a humanist sans
- **Accent**: Optional small serif for pull quotes or testimonials

### Color Direction

- Warm white or ivory base
- Deep warm brown or charcoal text
- Warm accent family such as terracotta, sage, or taupe
- Soft cream surfaces and gentle warm dividers

### Imagery Direction

- Natural, warm lighting
- Candid, relatable moments
- Human connection and form over perfection
- Avoid overly polished spa clichés

### Spacing and Layout

- Open but slightly more layered than the minimalist direction
- Comfortable section rhythm
- Spacious container widths with clear hierarchy

### Motion and Interaction

- Smooth, moderate transitions
- Gentle color shifts on hover
- Subtle motion only

### Why This Direction Fits Pilates

- Balances warmth with professionalism
- Supports the personal and community side of Pilates
- Feels timeless without being formal or stiff

### What to Avoid

- Overly saturated warm palettes
- Ornate typography
- Too many accent colors
- Wellness clichés and decorative clutter

### Reference Sites to Study

1. Upscale independent wellness studios
2. Premium small-batch wellness brands
3. Craft-focused professional service sites with restrained warmth

## Direction 3: Strong Modern

### Mood

- **Keywords**: Contemporary, empowering, confident, dynamic, grounded
- **Tone**: Progressive and professional; emphasizes strength and transformation
- **Impression**: Modern studio that understands both the science and the power of the practice
- **Best for**: Studios that want a sharper, more movement-forward identity

### Typography

- **Headings**: Bold contemporary sans-serif such as Inter Bold or DM Sans
- **Body**: Modern humanist sans with strong readability
- **Accent**: Optional mono or high-contrast label style for small UI elements

### Color Direction

- Off-white base
- Strong charcoal or dark navy text
- More assertive accent such as clay, sage, or bronze

### Imagery Direction

- Sharp, clean, well-lit movement photography
- Strong postures and dynamic mid-movement shots
- Clear, confident framing

### Spacing and Layout

- Purposeful spacing, slightly denser than the minimalist direction
- Flexible 12-column system
- Clear hierarchy and directional flow

### Motion and Interaction

- Crisp, purposeful transitions
- Slightly more noticeable hover feedback
- Still respectful of reduced-motion preferences

### Why This Direction Fits Pilates

- Feels progressive and energizing
- Supports strength, transformation, and modern instruction styles
- Communicates confidence without aggressive fitness branding

### What to Avoid

- Trend-chasing color or layout choices
- Loud typography or aggressive marketing tone
- Neon accents
- Overly geometric imagery that loses the human element

### Reference Sites to Study

1. Contemporary independent fitness studios
2. Progressive wellness or health-tech brands
3. Modern luxury brands with restrained boldness

## Decision Framework

Use these questions to choose the direction:

1. Who is the primary audience?
2. What is the studio’s positioning?
3. What feeling should the site create on first visit?
4. What visual treatment feels most authentic to the actual studio culture?
